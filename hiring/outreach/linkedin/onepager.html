Expansion Lead

AI Traffic Enforcement · Crashfree India
For builders driven by impact, not titles.
Gurugram

You will lead the expansion of our AI enforcement tool, built with Cars24 and run with traffic police. It tells officers which vehicle to stop, and why, in about ten seconds, and it is live in three cities. Your job is to take it across states and districts, and make it work on the ground.

There is no set playbook. You will build it.

What you will do:

  • Set the expansion plan: which states and districts, in what order.
  • Sign partnerships with police, transport departments and state governments.
  • Run each launch with our tech team, and stay until officers use it every day.
  • Track the data, fix what isn’t working, and decide where to go next.

What you need to bring:

  • Experience working with government: police, district or state administration.
  • Something you have built or run yourself. Failures count.
  • Comfort with data and technology.
  • A track record of choosing impact over titles.
  • Patience with slow processes, and the persistence to see them through.

What you get:

  • A small team, funding and Cars24’s tech.
  • A direct line to Cars24 and Crashfree India leadership.

How to apply:

Email gajendra@crashfreeindia.org with the subject Expansion Lead. Send your CV and a 4-minute video on a problem India looks away from, and why it matters.
