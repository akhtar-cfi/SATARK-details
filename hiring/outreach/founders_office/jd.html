Founder’s Office

New Initiatives · Crashfree India
Take an idea to a working pilot in weeks.
Then do it again.
Gurugram

You will work directly with the founders to turn new ideas into things that work. Your first one: tech-led ways to audit the public systems meant to keep India’s roads safe, and find out with data where they work and where they fail.

No team waiting for you, no fixed playbook. A problem, the tools, and room to move fast.

What you will do:

  • Prototype fast: a product, a research study, a partnership or an internal tool.
  • Build AI agents and tools that do real work for the team.
  • Research a problem and turn it into a clear call: what it means, and what we do next.
  • Find and set up the partnerships a new initiative needs to get off the ground.
  • Ship, learn, revise. Version one is meant to be rough.

What you need to bring:

  • AI agents you have built yourself (Claude Code, Codex or similar), not just chats with an assistant.
  • Research skills that go past information to insight, and the judgement to know what is worth saying.
  • High agency: you solve problems nobody assigned you.
  • Meticulous and organised. Fast never means sloppy.
  • Comfort owning a problem end to end before there is a team around it.
  • You change your mind when the evidence does, and your methods when they stop working.

What you get:

  • A playground for impact: real problems, real data and room to try things.
  • A direct line to Crashfree India and Cars24 leadership.
  • The chance to build your own track in the vertical you care about most.

How to apply:

Send your CV and one thing you have built (a link, a repo or a short demo) to akhtar@crashfreeindia.org, with “Founder’s Office” in the subject line.
