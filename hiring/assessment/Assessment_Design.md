# SATARK Pod Lead — Assessment Design

**Goal:** test the four things the note demands — agency/ownership, public-service intent, diligence with government material, persuasion — in under 10 days end-to-end (offer by ~8 Oct for a mid-Oct join).

## Funnel (5 gates, ~10 days)

### Gate 0 — Application screen (built into the outreach email)
The email asks for two things: **a CV** and **a 4–5 minute conviction-pitch video** (link) — a problem the candidate is genuinely passionate about, that India ignores, argued with data and anticipating counter-questions. The email seeds four example prompts (footpaths as no city's KPI · child helmets · the golden hour · school-toilet maintenance) to set the register; candidates may go anywhere, roads or not. *(v2 change, 24 Sep: replaces the earlier 200-word reply; CV is now required.)*
- **Kills:** copy-paste applications, pure exam-toppers with nothing run, people who can't hold a camera and an argument for 4 minutes.
- **Video scorecard (1–5 each, need ≥18/25):** conviction · data rigor · anticipating counterarguments · clarity · time discipline (did they respect 4–5 min?).
- **CV read:** cross-check against the PRATIBHA Setu bio and the screening flags; verify founder/leadership claims on LinkedIn.
- Time cost: ~10 min/application. Advance ~30–40%.

### Gate 1 — 30-min call (Akhtar or Deepanshu)
Not an interview — a conversation to test the room presence a DCP will experience.
- Ask them to explain their "thing they ran" and push back twice ("why didn't that work?", "what would you do differently?"). Watch for defensiveness vs. ownership.
- One knowledge probe: "What do you know about why challans go unpaid?" — tests whether they researched us before the call.
- Gettability check, straight: notice period, family/location, next UPSC attempt plans. **Ask directly whether they will attempt CSE 2027.** (Policy call needed — see Open Questions.)

### Gate 2 — The assignment (48 hours, 2 pages + 5-min video; shortlisted candidates only)
> **The 90-Day District Plan.** Pick any district you know personally (not Jaipur/Bengaluru/Gurugram). You have SATARK, Cars24's funding, and nothing else — no warm introduction. In 2 pages: (1) whom you would meet, in what order, and why that order; (2) the first 10 minutes of your first meeting with the SP/DCP — what you actually say; (3) what will stall you, and what you do in week 6 when nobody has replied; (4) what "live" means and by which day. Then record a 5-minute phone video: pitch SATARK to a skeptical Additional SP who thinks this is a marketing gimmick by a used-car company.

Why this design:
- The 2-pager tests **diligence + sequencing** (do they discover DRSC/Collector/Transport Commissioner structures themselves? do they read the SC order we attached?).
- The video tests **the exact failure mode Deepanshu named**: whether an officer would smell "sales." Selling ability without sales stench.
- "District you know personally" tests groundedness; "week 6 silence" tests patience planning, not fantasy.
- 48h deadline tests bias-to-action; mid-Oct hire allows no month-long take-homes.

**Scorecard (1–5 each, need ≥18/25):** sequencing realism · government literacy · persuasion without selling · patience/stall plan · clarity of writing.

### Gate 3 — Live role-play + grilling (in person, Gurugram, half day)
1. **Role-play (30 min):** someone plays a DCP who (a) is interested but cites "data privacy issues", (b) asks who pays, (c) goes cold and checks their phone. Yuvraj or Yashvardhan plays it — they have sat in these rooms. Score recovery, not polish.
2. **Assignment grill (30 min):** attack their own plan. "Your SP transfers out in month 2 — now what?" Founder mindset shows in how they defend/adapt.
3. **Mission depth (Deepanshu/Akhtar, 30 min):** why this over another attempt, over a corporate job; what they'd do in year 3.
4. Optional same-day: coffee with Yuvraj — would the current pod work for/with this person?

### Gate 4 — Founders' round (Gajju + Pushkal, 45 min)
Culture, aggression of ambition (Vikram's "impossible goal" bar), comp closure, start date.

## Rejection hygiene
Every candidate who did the assignment gets a personal 3-line reply. This pool talks to each other (UPSC circles are tight); how we reject is marketing for the next hire.

## Open questions (decide before Gate 1 calls start)
1. **Future UPSC attempts:** allowed alongside the job or not? My recommendation: be explicit that the role cannot absorb a 4-month prep leave; someone planning a serious 2027 attempt is an orange flag for a "own it like a founder" seat. But do not auto-reject — several strong profiles will be attempt-ambivalent, and the mission can convert them.
2. **One hire or two:** the operating plan says "Yuvraj + two hires." If two, Gate 3 can also grade for the second BD seat — one funnel, two offers.
3. **Comp band:** needed before Gate 1 (candidates will ask). Pool's current comp: ₹25k–₹4.4L/month.
4. **Who plays the DCP:** fix one person for consistency across candidates.
